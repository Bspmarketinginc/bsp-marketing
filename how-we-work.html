Step by Step

How We Get Started

01
Discovery

We Sit Down and Listen

Every partnership starts with a real conversation — no sales pitch, no pressure. We meet with you in person or virtually to get a clear picture of your business, your products or services, your target audience, and what you've already tried. This is your time to tell us everything.

02
Goal Setting

We Define What You Want and When

Together, we lock in your goals and build a timeline around them. Whether you want to grow your social media presence in 30 days, launch a campaign by a specific date, or hit a sales target by end of quarter — we map it all out so everyone is aligned from day one.

03
Strategy & Onboarding

We Build Your Custom Plan

Once we understand your goals and timeline, we put together a tailored marketing strategy built specifically for your business. We collect account access, brand materials, and everything else we need — then get to work. No templates, no cookie-cutter campaigns.

04
Execution

We Execute Across Every Channel

With the plan locked in, we get to work — managing your social media, running campaigns, creating content, and handling every moving part of your marketing. You stay focused on running your business while we handle the growth.

05
Ongoing

We Keep You in the Loop — Every Week

You'll never be left wondering what we're doing or how things are going. Every week, we send you a clear update covering what was done, what's coming next, and how your results are tracking. If something needs to change, we catch it fast and adjust.

Staying Connected

How We Communicate

We believe great marketing requires great communication. From the moment you sign with us, you'll always know who to call, how to reach us, and exactly what's happening with your account.

We don't hide behind automated reports or generic email blasts. We talk to you like a real partner — clearly, consistently, and always with your goals in mind.
