CLIENT SERVICE AGREEMENT

Confidential — For Client Use Only

This Marketing Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on                     , by and between BSP Marketing Inc. ("Agency") and                      ("Client").

1. Services

BSP Marketing Inc. agrees to provide marketing services, including but not limited to:

  • Social Media Management
  • Content Creation
  • Paid Advertising Management
  • Branding & Strategy
  • Email Marketing
  • Website Optimization
  • Other services agreed upon in writing

2. Term

This Agreement begins on                  and shall continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party with thirty (30) days written notice.

3. Payment

  • Client agrees to pay BSP Marketing Inc. $         per month.
  • Invoices are due within seven (7) days of receipt.
  • Accounts more than fifteen (15) days overdue may have services paused until payment is received.
  • Late balances may accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.
  • Client agrees to pay all reasonable collection costs, including attorney fees and court costs.

4. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

  • Provide account access when needed.
  • Supply requested information promptly.
  • Review and approve content in a timely manner.
  • Comply with applicable laws and platform policies.

5. Intellectual Property

  • Ownership of final deliverables transfers to Client only after full payment has been received.
  • BSP Marketing Inc. retains ownership of all templates, systems, processes, methodologies, and proprietary materials.

6. Results Disclaimer

BSP Marketing Inc. does not guarantee any specific increase in sales, leads, revenue, followers, engagement, website traffic, rankings, or return on investment.

7. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all confidential information private and not disclose it to third parties without written consent.

8. Portfolio Rights

Client grants BSP Marketing Inc. permission to display completed work, logos, testimonials, and performance results for portfolio and marketing purposes unless otherwise agreed in writing.

9. Revisions

  • Monthly service packages include two (2) rounds of revisions unless otherwise stated.
  • Additional revisions may be billed separately.

10. Termination

  • Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice.
  • If Client cancels before the completion of an agreed service term, Client agrees to pay an early termination fee equal to one month's service fee.
  • All outstanding invoices remain due immediately upon termination.

11. Limitation of Liability

BSP Marketing Inc.'s total liability shall not exceed the amount paid by Client during the three (3) months preceding any claim.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
