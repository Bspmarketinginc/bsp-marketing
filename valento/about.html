Valento

Est. 2019 — Milan

Fewer things,
made properly.

Valento started with two people, one wool merchant and a stubborn belief that a wardrobe should get better with age rather than cheaper by the season.

The beginning

A workroom above a fabric shop.

We spent our first year not making clothes at all. We visited mills, argued about weights and finishes, and filled a room with cloth we loved. Only then did we start cutting.

That order of operations is still how every Valento season is built. The fabric decides the garment — not a trend forecast, and not a calendar that demands twelve drops a year.

Seven years on, we're a team of eleven across Milan and New York, working with four mills and two ateliers we have used since the beginning.

Materials

What we use, and why.

Every fabric in the collection has to pass three tests: it has to feel better after a year, it has to come from a mill that will tell us where the fibre started, and it has to be repairable.

Wool & cashmere

Woven in Biella, Italy, from certified non-mulesed wool and grade-A Mongolian cashmere. Long fibres, tighter spin, far less pilling.

Linen & cotton

European flax and long-staple cotton, garment-washed in Portugal so the piece you receive has already done its shrinking.

Silk & leather

Mulberry silk at 19 momme or heavier, and vegetable-tanned Tuscan leather from a tannery working without chromium.

Timeline

Seven years, slowly.

We have grown at the pace our makers can grow. It is not the fastest way to build a clothing brand, but it is the only way we know how to keep the quality where it started.

  • 2019

    One coat, one colour

    We launched with 120 wool overcoats and a website built in a weekend. They sold out in eleven days.

  • 2021

    The knitwear programme

    A partnership with a family knitting mill outside Florence gave us cashmere we could stand behind.

  • 2023

    Repairs for life

    We opened a mending bench in Milan. Everything we have ever made can come back to it.

  • 2026

    Collection 04

    Twelve pieces, four mills, and the first season made entirely from traceable fibre.
