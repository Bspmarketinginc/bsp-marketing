The making

We buy the cloth before we draw the clothes.

Every Valento season starts at the mill, not the sketchbook. We choose a handful of fabrics we would happily wear for a decade, then design the smallest number of pieces that do them justice.

It means fewer styles, longer lead times and no midseason drops. It also means the coat you buy this year is the same coat we will still be repairing in ten.

How we work

Standards

Three rules we don't bend.

They are unglamorous, and they are the whole business.

Natural fibres first

Wool, cashmere, linen, cotton and silk. Where a synthetic earns its place — stretch in a trouser, a coat lining — we say so on the product page.

Runs of 300 or fewer

We make what we expect to sell and restock the pieces that last. Nothing is discounted into a landfill at the end of the season.

Repairs, not replacements

Send anything back for mending at cost, for as long as you own it. Buttons, seams, moth holes, worn cuffs.
