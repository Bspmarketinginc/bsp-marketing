Shipping & returns

Complimentary shipping over $250, worldwide. Orders leave Milan within two working days; expect 2–4 days in Europe, 4–7 elsewhere.

Returns are free within 30 days, unworn with tags attached. Refunds land within five working days of arrival.

Repairs

Our mending bench repairs anything we've made, for as long as you own it, charged at the cost of materials and postage. Buttons, seams, moth holes, worn cuffs and elbows.
